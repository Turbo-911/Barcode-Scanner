                    QA BARCODE TEST IMAGES SUITE
================================================================================

All test BMP files in this directory are valid Interleaved 2 of 5 (ITF) barcodes
matching the team's ITF encoding specification (src/encoder.cpp and README.md).
They are 8-bit grayscale BMPs directly readable by loadBMP (src/BMP.cpp).
